Concept images the owner supplied for the eagle-eye round. They set the look for rooms rebuilt in Blender: seen from high above at an angle, with convincing architecture, dense period furniture, warm candle and lamp pools against cold moonlight, glossy dark floors, Persian rugs, and dust sheets.
